Purpose

TruthLens is an educational tool designed to help users identify linguistic and structural signals often associated with misleading or highly biased news content. It promotes critical thinking in the digital age.

Architecture

Built completely in core Java without external dependencies. It utilizes a custom lexical analyzer, object-oriented scoring algorithms, and a custom lightweight HTTP server to serve this interface.

How it works

The system evaluates text against predefined heuristics: capitalization ratios, punctuation density, presence of emotional or suspicious keywords, and sentence structures to generate a cumulative risk score.

Limitations

TruthLens performs a textual analysis only. It does not check facts against databases, verify sources, or understand deep context. A high risk score means the writing style is suspicious, not necessarily that the facts are false.

Understanding Risk Scores

The TruthLens risk score ranges from 0 to 100.

  • 0 - 30
    Potentially Credible: The text uses standard, neutral journalistic language.
  • 31 - 60
    Needs Verification: The text contains some emotional or sensational elements. Verify claims.
  • 61 - 100
    Potentially Misleading: High density of suspicious language, extreme claims, or manipulative tactics.

    • Safe Information Practices

    • Check the Source: Is it a reputable news organization?
    • Look for Corroboration: Are other major outlets reporting the same story?
    • Read Beyond the Headline: Headlines are often designed to provoke clicks.
    • Check the Date: Old stories are sometimes shared out of context as new events.
